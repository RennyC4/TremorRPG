^5Welcome to my humble weapons and armor shop!^7

Ah, I’d have a grander selection if the roads weren’t crawling with bandits these days. Every shipment is a gamble, and what does
arrive is often snatched up by eager locals before I can even place it on the shelves. Strange times, indeed—but survival always
demands a keen blade and a sturdy shield.

I’ve seen warriors rise and fall, and I’ve learned one thing: a fair price is worth more than a cheap sword. These pieces are
honest, tried, and true. I refuse to change my prices, even when the world turns sour. Choose wisely, traveller, for what you carry may
well decide your fate on the road ahead.
