Forest raised me, not people. My father cut trees, his father before him — and now here I stand, still feeding the world with timber.
I know every sound a tree makes before it falls. Every lie bad wood tells before it breaks. This town’s bones? Half of them came
from my logs. I don’t sell rot. I don’t sell stories. Just straight lumber and honest weight.

You want gold, you bring wood.. simple as that.
